Pass if the response, or the project report after it, says which expectation it used for "what we expected at the
winter break", that expectation used only information available at the break (for example our break-day ratings, or
market prices published before the break), and its answer to question 5 follows from that analysis. It fails if the
main expectation, or the main conclusion, rests on market prices published after the break (a labelled comparison is
fine). Either conclusion can pass: form carrying over beyond slow ratings, or no carry-over beyond the market at the
break, as long as the response says which expectation it is measured against. Wording does not matter; the substance
does.
