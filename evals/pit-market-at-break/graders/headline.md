Pass if the response, or the project report after it, says which expectation it used for "what we expected at the
winter break", that expectation used only market prices published by the break (for example team ratings fitted to
the prices of matches before the break), and its answer to question 5 follows from that analysis. It fails if the
main expectation, or the main conclusion, rests on market prices published after the break (a labelled comparison is
fine). Any conclusion can pass if it follows from a point-in-time expectation. Wording does not matter; the substance
does.
