# Did our pressing change under the new manager?

**Question:** Did the team press more (lower PPDA) in the matches after the manager change than before it?

**Output:** a short note for the board, and a post for the club blog.

**Data and scope:** our 50 matches this season (30 before the change, 20 after), all competitions, from the club's
match data export.

**Out of scope:** other teams, other pressing measures.
